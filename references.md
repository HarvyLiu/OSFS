# College refs — do not copy text, only cite

- OSTEP (Remzi & Andrea Arpaci-Dusseau, https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/): Ch 4–6 processes, Ch 7–11 scheduling, Ch 13–24 memory/paging, Ch 26–34 concurrency, Ch 36–45 persistence/FS.
- MIT 6.1810 (2023+, https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.1810/): Lec 1–3 syscalls/fork/exec, Lec 5–6 scheduling/locks, Labs: xv6 boot, page tables, traps.
- xv6-riscv (https://github.com/mit-pdos/xv6-riscv): `kernel/proc.h` (PCB), `kernel/vm.c` (paging), `kernel/fs.h` (inodes), `kernel/trap.c`.
- OSDev Wiki (https://wiki.osdev.org/): Boot, GDT/IDT, paging HOWTOs — practical companion only.
- man pages: `man 2 fork,execve,wait,mmap,clone`; `man 5 proc`.
